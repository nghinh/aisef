## Old AC-4 (invalid as a story-proving criterion)

## New AC-4 (authoritative)

> Given a ledger whose final record is otherwise fully valid but whose final newline is missing, when `python -m ledgerlock repair-tail <ledger>` is executed in a subprocess, then the exit code is `0`, stdout is empty, the command appends exactly one missing `\n`, no other byte is changed, no committed record is removed, and a subsequent `verify()` reports the ledger clean (`{"ok": True, "first_bad_index": None}`).

This criterion is RED against the parent stub (it does not repair the missing newline) and GREEN only when the `repair-tail` subcommand is wired to `Ledger.repair_tail` (STORY-03-02 semantics, owner arbitration of 2026-09-16).
