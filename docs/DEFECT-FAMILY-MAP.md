# Defect family map (systematic hardening v1, Phase 2)

Rendered from `closure-evidence/hardening/defect-family-map.json` by `validation/render_hardening_docs.py` — edit the JSON. A defect family is one architectural cause; defect ids that share it are not independent, and the unit of closure is the family. Invariant ids refer to `docs/INVARIANTS.md`.

## Families

| family | name | cause | invariants | members |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FAM-IDENTITY | Identity by recency or position | A control decision selects its evidence by chronological recency (`last`, newest, HEAD) or by position/spelling instead of by an exact identity (story epoch, candidate, baseline root, artifact digest, cohort directory). | INV-D, INV-E, INV-C, INV-R | D-004, D-021, D-023, D-033, lỗi-42 |
| FAM-OUTCOME | Outcome misclassification (absence / unrunnable read as pass or fail) | An absence, an environment failure or a non-verdict is mapped onto the two-valued pass/fail axis instead of a typed outcome (UNRUNNABLE, UNCONFIGURED, NOT_APPLICABLE, INFRA, HUMAN_REQUIRED). | INV-F, INV-N, INV-T | D-001, D-005, D-008, D-010, D-012, D-029 |
| FAM-PROSE | Prose parsed into control state | Free-form model or tool text is matched by substring/regex and the match creates control state, outranking or standing in for a structured source. | INV-F, INV-Q | D-018, D-026 |
| FAM-RETRY | Retry locality and budget accounting | A failure of one stage (reviewer, provider, tool, environment) is routed to a retry of another stage or charged to the wrong budget; the loop's only recovery path is 'open a developer session again'. | INV-G, INV-H | D-006, D-032 |
| FAM-RECOVERY | Recovery and resume semantics | A resume or recovery action recomputes or changes evaluated state (baseline, worktree contents, candidate) without the evidence that depended on the previous state being invalidated or refreshed — or leaves the state unrecovered for the next attempt. | INV-K, INV-E, INV-C, INV-B | D-034, D-035, OBS-R13 |
| FAM-OWNERSHIP | Workspace ownership and generated files | Paths written by the harness, tools, generated client config or pre-existing dirt are attributed to the developer/story (or blamed on the wrong owner), so scope checks, freezes, diffs and merges act on files nobody authored. | INV-I, INV-J | D-011, D-030, OBS-OC-merge, lỗi-38/37 |
| FAM-TOOL | Tool declared but not available | A configured or inferred verification command is trusted to be runnable in the declared execution environment; its absence is read as a behavioural result. | INV-N, INV-F | D-009, D-013, D-028, F-9, Run2-B |
| FAM-PROCESS | Process lifecycle and liveness | A parent process, lock or claim is treated as the whole: children survive their parent's death, a claim survives its process, cleanup races a writer. | INV-L, INV-M | D-024, D-025, D-027, lỗi-15/13 |
| FAM-SCHEDULER | Scheduler uses declared scope, not effective scope | Two rules for one truth: the scheduler partitions waves by declared write_scope while the guard grants additional shared paths. | INV-O, INV-J | D-031 |
| FAM-APPROVAL | Approval and pre-registration binding | A human decision (approval, frozen rule, acceptance scope) is bound to memory, mtime or an implicit default rather than to an exact artifact digest or an explicit declaration. | INV-P, INV-Q | D-014, D-017, lỗi-25/28 |
| FAM-RELEASE | Release and closure identity | Product identity is conflated with repository HEAD, platform-dependent spellings or a second implementation of the same digest, so recording evidence or moving platforms changes 'what was released'. | INV-R | D-007, D-019, D-020, D-022, REPLAY-DRIFT |
| FAM-BENCH | Benchmark integrity | Bench artifacts, cohorts and stop rules leak across directories/experiments or depend on the operator; infrastructure failures are written as results. | INV-R, INV-F | D-003, D-015, D-016 |
| FAM-JUDGE | Model output as control authority | A model verdict is the sole basis of a blocking decision or a plan conflict, or model failure is indistinguishable from framework misclassification. | INV-F, INV-Q, INV-T | D-002, F-7, OBS-OC-2, AC4-ARBITRATION |

## Reading the map

- **Open in the register**: D-002 (P2, FAM-JUDGE), D-003 (P2, FAM-BENCH), D-011 (P2, FAM-OWNERSHIP), D-024 (P2, FAM-PROCESS), D-035 (P1, FAM-RECOVERY). Phase 8 dispositions each as FIXED, NOT_A_DEFECT with proof, or SUPERSEDED by a family fix.
- **Chains**: D-032 → D-033 → D-034 → D-035 are four symptoms of two causes — identity by recency (FAM-IDENTITY) and recovery without evidence invalidation (FAM-RECOVERY). Each narrow release removed one symptom and exposed the next; the family fix is the kernel's evidence identity + freshness model (Phases 4, 8, 9).
- **Previous fixes marked local** are the sibling risk the Phase 3 sweep targets (`closure-evidence/hardening/sibling-scan.json`).
